# Connectors and bindings

Hand-written sync code wired into types. To add one, see [writing-a-connector.md](writing-a-connector.md).

- **Connectors** (`services/congress/src/connectors/`): hand-written sync code with its own cache DB (`CONNECTORS_DATA_DIR/<name>.sqlite3`), declared with `defineConnector` (`contract.ts`): a source schema (kinds, fields, email keys, per-record **facts** such as `editable`/`canRsvp`), `sync` (pull, run by `registry.ts`'s scheduler), `read`, optional `push` (create/update/delete/`act`), a setup-panel Hono app at `/congress/connectors/<name>/*`, and `onEvent` for `google.account_*`. A connector never writes type records; it emits source changes (`runtime.ts`'s `onSourceChange`) that bindings pull in, and resolves people through `ctx.people.resolve` (`lookupOrCreate` on Person). Its `googleScopes` join the Google connect union. Setup lives in Settings → Connectors (`frontend/src/connectors/panels.tsx`).
  - **Bindings** (`typeEngine/bindings/`, phase 6) wire a connector's source kind into a type, as part of its definition (ops `set_binding`/`remove_binding`, checked against the running connector's schema at publish; premades skip that check).
    - **Fields:** `sync` fields are pulled and pushed back; `pull` fields are read-only on bound records; unbound fields stay local. Unmapped source values are read live (`withBinding` → `RecordDto.binding`).
    - **Pull:** a pull applies only what the source changed since the record's shadow (`binding_shadows`), so pending local edits and push echoes survive. Rich text keeps its chips while its plain projection still matches. The first pull is a quiet backfill (no events; time triggers seed silently). Records outlive the source's window; only an emitted delete removes one.
    - **Push:** owner/AI edits push through `binding_outbox` (retry with backoff; a `ConnectorRefusedError` reverts to the source and shows as failed).
      - Changing the `create` target field creates, moves (same record id) or detaches.
      - Source changes are held while a create is in flight.
    - **Guard:** `beforeRecordWrite` refuses locked fields (`lock` fact false, or the source no longer has it) and `delete: "never"`. Actions (`POST /congress/records/:id/actions/:action`) call `push.act` when their `when`/`unless` facts hold.
  - **Event** (premade) replaced the Calendar Chamber (2026-09-30: local events, "not attending" as Hide, rich text, links, old `event-…` ids as aliases, event settings and pins; the importer and its `import:calendar` dry run are in git history). A hidden type publishes no events. Its binding syncs title/time/place/description/calendar, pulls your response and linked People, locks others' invitations (`editable` fact) and offers Accept/Maybe/Decline (real RSVPs); Hide is a local toggle. The connector's times are instants (all-day = midnight in the owner's zone, end exclusive); changing the calendar field creates/moves/detaches.
  - **Calendar views** (`frontend/src/views/calendar/`): Timeline at `/events` and Week at `/events/week`, hand-written over Event records (`GET /congress/records?type=event&from=&to=`), with calendar colors and account problems from the connector's status. `views/coreViews.ts` lists Congress's own views next to Chambers' (Search, pinned views); they appear once their type is visible.
  - **Live content and search** (phase 7): a connector may offer `read.detail` (live content for a record, e.g. a thread's bodies: `GET /congress/records/:id/live`, the AI's `read_<slug>`), `read.search` (the whole source, `search_all_<plural>`) and `read.fetch` (one item into its cache, so `materialize` makes it a record on demand). It publishes its own events with `ctx.publish` (declared in `events`, so Settings → Logs lists them), adds AI tools with `tools`, and `emitChange(..., quiet)` pulls a backfill without events. A type whose bindings can't create (`canCreate`) only gets records from its source.
  - **Gmail** (`gmail/`) is the Email type's source and replaced the Mail Chamber (2026-09-30; its `import:mail` dry run and importer are in git history): one cached row per thread (never windowed; 30-day backfill, then Gmail history, each touched thread re-read live), bodies/HTML/attachments live (`frontend/src/connectors/live/GmailThread.tsx`, a sandboxed CSP-locked iframe with remote images blocked until tapped), Mark read (on open) as its only write, never send or delete. It publishes `mail.received` (Primary only unless its panel says every category) and creates People only from the **To** of mail the owner sent; everyone else just links. Old `thread-<acct>:<thread>` ids are aliases and `/mail/t/...` redirects.
  - **Hevy** and **Apple Health** (`hevy/`, `health/`) replaced the Fitness Chamber (2026-09-30; old `workout-<id>`/`routine-<hevyId>` ids are aliases, `/fitness/...` redirects; the importer and its `import:fitness` dry run are in git history). Hevy fills Workout (pull-only; sets and 1RMs read live, `frontend/src/connectors/live/HevyWorkout.tsx`) and Routine (a rename goes back to Hevy; exercises are edited in `HevyRoutine.tsx` / `hevy_set_routine_exercises`; a new one is made at `/e/new/routine` once it has an exercise, and never retried, since Hevy can't delete a routine). Health is a time series (`read.series`), never records, filled by Health Auto Export through the unauthenticated `hooks()` webhook (the Shortcut's old `/api/fitness/health/ingest` reaches it via `callHook`), drawn by the core Health view (`/fitness/health`, a feed card through the connector's `feed()`).
  - **Location** (`location/`) replaced the Map Chamber (2026-10-01; old `place-<id>` ids are aliases and `/map/...` redirects; the importer and its `import:map` dry run are in git history). The Map is Congress's own views: `/map` (the day's places and trips, Leaflet; the feed card "Today") and `/map/pending` (stops to classify: a new place is a Place record made there, whose rebuild confirms the stop), in `frontend/src/views/map/`; Traccar and the tunables live in its Settings → Connectors panel. It is the Map Chamber's tracking engine (positions kept forever; visits and trips rebuilt from them, the owner's classifications carried across) reading the owner's **Place** records (hidden until then) through `ctx.records.list` and `onRecordChange`: a Place made, moved or deleted rebuilds that stretch of history. Place's latitude/longitude/radius are plain number fields paired into one map picker by `set_map_point` (`layout.mapPoint`). Its feed (`feed()`: groups per source) is today's map card under "map" and "You're here" as a Place record under "e".
  - **WhatsApp** (`whatsapp/`) replaced the WhatsApp Chamber (2026-10-01; there was nothing to import, `/whatsapp/...` URLs are unchanged, the Chats view keeps its pin): wraps the wa-reader socket (`readerClient.ts`, whose only POSTs are pairing and the local mark-read; `readonly.test.ts` keeps it so). One Chat record per chat the reader knows, synced every 2 minutes and pulled quietly (chat activity is too frequent to announce); messages stay in the reader. `Muted` is the phone's own mute (reader `muted_until` from app-state `Mute` events and history sync; timed mutes expire on their own), read-only here, and keeps a chat out of the feed. People by phone (`people.find/resolve` take `{ email?, phone? }`): only a 1:1 chat the owner wrote in at least five times (of its last 100 messages) may create one (`createPeople`, on by default, a checkbox in its Settings → Connectors panel, which also holds the QR pairing). The views are Congress's own (`frontend/src/views/whatsapp/`: `/whatsapp` the chat list, `/whatsapp/c/:jid` a chat, read-only, marking read locally when seen) and a Chat record shows its newest messages live (`connectors/live/WhatsappChat.tsx`). AI tools `whatsapp_*`.
  - **Google Calendar** (`googleCalendar/`) is the Event type's source. It caches selected calendars over ±180 days (sync tokens, 410 → full resync), with every guest. Guests never create People (being on an invitation isn't contact): each sync links unlinked guests to existing People by email (`ctx.people.find`). A Person is only ever created from **direct contact** - the owner or the AI, or later a connector that sees the owner wrote to someone themselves (Mail, WhatsApp; evidence `corresponded`). Event's binding pushes edits, moves and answers through it; the AI's calendar tools are the generic `/mcp/types` ones (`list_events` with `from`/`to`, `decline_event`, ...).
